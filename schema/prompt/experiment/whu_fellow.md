 


## Define

`whu_fellow(X, Y)` means **Y is the immediate logical predecessor or provider for X** in the paper’s workflow/argument (X occurs after or depends on Y).
**Do not use the superclass `whu:ActivityStep`** as subject or object—only the specific classes below.

## Allowed Schema Signatures (only these)

1. `(b:whu_SpecimenColleciton) -[:whu_fellow]-> (a:whu_EnvironmentFeature)`
2. `(c:whu_SpecimenPreprocessing) -[:whu_fellow]-> (b:whu_SpecimenColleciton)`
3. `(d:whu_Bio_chemical_Experiment) -[:whu_fellow]-> (c:whu_SpecimenPreprocessing)`
4. `(e:whu_Computational_Experiment) -[:whu_fellow]-> (d:whu_Bio_chemical_Experiment)`

## Context (when to extract)

Extract `X -[:whu_fellow]-> Y` when the text shows:

* **Temporal cues**: *after, then, next, subsequently, following, prior to, before, step n → step n+1*.
* **Dependency cues**: X **uses** specimens/data/results **from** Y (e.g., “using data produced by…”, “processed specimen was then …”).
* **Environment anchoring (case 1 only)**: collection explicitly tied to a site/feature (*at, from, in, sampled at/from*).

## Decision Rules

1. **Direction**: “X after Y / following Y, X / then X” ⇒ `X -[:whu_fellow]-> Y`.
2. **Dependency ⇒ order**: If X consumes outputs (specimens/data) from Y ⇒ `X -[:whu_fellow]-> Y`.
3. **Type guard**: Only emit edges matching the **four signatures** above; never use `ActivityStep` directly.
4. **Deduplicate**: Keep one edge per (X, Y) per passage.
5. **Conflicts**: Prefer statements with **stronger temporal wording** or **explicit input/output dependency**.

 
## Examples

**Result 1**
• Relation: `(b:whu_SpecimenColleciton) -[:whu_fellow]-> (a:whu_EnvironmentFeature)`
• Extraction:
    ◦ Head (X): `whu_SpecimenColleciton` {name: "collection of the surface layer (0-20 cm) of soil"}
    ◦ Tail (Y): `whu_EnvironmentFeature` {name: "National Purple Soil Fertility and Fertilizer Benefit onitoring Base of SWU"}
• Justification: This extraction fits the first schema, where a collection event is anchored to a specific environmental feature. The text uses the dependency cue "collected from" to explicitly link the action of collecting soil to its source location.
• Supporting Text: "...the surface layer (0-20 cm) of soil was collected from the National Purple Soil Fertility and Fertilizer Benefit onitoring Base of SWU".

**Result 2**
• Relation: `(c:whu_SpecimenPreprocessing) -[:whu_fellow]-> (b:whu_SpecimenColleciton)`
• Extraction:
    ◦ Head (X): `whu_SpecimenPreprocessing` {name: "air-drying and passing through a 20-mesh sieve"}
    ◦ Tail (Y): `whu_SpecimenColleciton` {name: "collection of the surface layer (0-20 cm) of soil"}
• Justification: This extraction demonstrates a sequential workflow. The paper first describes the soil collection. The immediately following sentence describes how "The soils" were processed, clearly indicating that preprocessing follows collection. This establishes a direct temporal and logical sequence.
• Supporting Text: "...the surface layer (0-20 cm) of soil was collected... The soils were air-dried and passed through a 20-mesh sieve".

**Result 3**
• Relation: `(e:whu_Computational_Experiment) -[:whu_fellow]-> (d:whu_Bio_chemical_Experiment)`
• Extraction:
    ◦ Head (X): `whu_Computational_Experiment` {name: "statistical analysis"}
    ◦ Tail (Y): `whu_Bio_chemical_Experiment` {name: "Determination of THg and MeHg"}
• Justification: This extraction shows a dependency relationship. The statistical analysis (a computational experiment) was conducted on the "various parameters" that were generated by the biochemical experiments, such as the measurement of THg and MeHg content. The computational step logically follows and depends on the results of the chemical analysis.
• Supporting Text: "The THg content in soil and rice plants... was measured using F-732 cold vapor atomic fluorescence spectroscopy... For MeHg analysis, 0.2 g of soil or 0.1 g of rice plants were extracted... R version 4.1.0 was used for statistical analysis. The effects of different biochar treatments on various parameters were tested using the non-parametric Kruskal-Wallis test".
