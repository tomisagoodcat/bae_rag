 
### Define:

A **scientific dataset** that is either **generated by** or **used in** research activities.

* It is modeled as a subclass of **mp\:Data**, **dcat\:Dataset**, and **prov\:Entity**, and is aligned with **bfo\:GenericallyDependentContinuant** (indicating dependence on publications).
* **whu\:Data** participates in scientific argumentation: it can **support** or **challenge** `mp:Statement` / `mp:Claim` via `mp:support` or `mp:challenge`.
* **Provenance** is explicit: data is causally linked to `whu:ActivityStep` through `prov:wasGeneratedBy` or `prov:wasUsedBy`.
* A dataset may appear as a **table, figure, or file**. A part of a dataset (e.g., a **row in a table**) can also count as `whu:Data` if it independently supports or challenges a claim.

---

### Purpose:

* **Scientific argumentation**: A dataset can **support** or **challenge** a `mp:Claim` / `mp:Statement`.
* **Measurement linkage**: A dataset can link to `whu:ScalarMeasurementDatum`, which contains the **value**, **unit**, and **logical comparator**.
* **Method dependency**: A dataset is associated with a `whu:Method` that defines how the data was obtained.
* **Provenance in activities**: A research activity (`whu:ActivityStep`) may **use** a dataset (`prov:used`) and subsequently **generate** another dataset (`prov:generated`).

---

### Context:

Use this class **only when the text explicitly describes data as a research artifact**. Avoid speculative inference; rely on **explicit mentions** (e.g., dataset, table, figure, measurement) and unambiguous descriptions.

* **Argumentation role**: Trigger when a dataset **supports** or **challenges** a `whu:Claim` / `mp:Statement`.
* **Tables and figures**: If a **table or figure** is described but cannot be separated into smaller parts, treat it as a single `whu:Data`.
* **Scalar measurements**: When a `whu:ScalarMeasurementDatum` supports or challenges a claim but is not linked to an existing dataset, create a `whu:Data` node to connect it.
* **Research activities**: Trigger when a research activity (`whu:BioChemicalActivityStep`, `whu:ComputationalActivityStep`) **uses** a dataset (`prov:used`) and **generates** another dataset (`prov:generated`).

---
### Notes:

- If the original text of a `whu:Data` (dataset) explicitly contains a **numeric value** with a **unit** (and optionally a logical comparator, e.g., `>`, `<`, `=`), then create a corresponding `whu:ScalarMeasurementDatum`.  
- The `whu:ScalarMeasurementDatum` must:  
  - Store the **value** and **unit** (and comparator if present).  
  - Be linked to the parent `whu:Data` using `dcterms:hasPart`.  
- This ensures fine-grained representation of measurements within datasets.  



### Use semantic links:

* `whu:DataSet` → `mp:supports` → `mp:Claim`
* `whu:DataSet`  → `mp:supports` → `mp:Statement`
* `whu:DataSet` → `mp:challenges` → `mp:Claim`
* `whu:DataSet` → `mp:challenges` → `mp:Statement`
* `whu:DataSet` → `dcterms:hasPart` → `whu:ScalarMeasurementDatum`
* `whu:Method` → `mp:supports` → `whu:DataSet`
* `whu:BioChemicalActivityStep` / `whu:ComputationalActivityStep` → `prov:used` → `whu:DataSet`
* `whu:BioChemicalActivityStep` / `whu:ComputationalActivityStep` → `prov:generated` → `whu:DataSet`

---

### Examples:

> **whu\:Data 1** : Figure 4: MeHg accumulations in different tissues of rice plants in the three treatments.

* **Generated by / Used in research activities**: This dataset was generated from the measurements of MeHg concentrations in various tissues of rice plants (roots, stalks, leaves, and grains) collected at the mature stage after harvest.
* **Supports / Challenges claims**: This dataset supports the conclusion that the application of both original and modified biochar significantly decreased the bioaccumulation of MeHg in rice plants, particularly in hulls and grains, compared to the control. It also highlights that grains were the most cumulative part of the rice plant.

> **whu\:Data 2** : Porewater Dissolved Organic Matter (DOM) Characteristics

* **Generated / Used**: This dataset comprises measurements of DOM properties (Dissolved Organic Carbon (DOC) content, Specific Ultraviolet Absorbance SUVA(254), spectral slope SR, absorption coefficient a(355), modified humification index HIX, and autochthonous contribution index BIX) from porewater samples, obtained via optical analyses (fluorescence, UV-Vis) and a TOC analyzer.
* **Supports / Challenges claims**: This data supports that biochar significantly altered DOM characteristics, showing increased aromaticity, molecular weight, chromophoric components, and humic character. It also indicated enhanced microbial activity in biochar treatments, crucial for explaining observed MeHg production.
* **Provenance**: DOM characterization was conducted in the Environmental Biogeochemistry Laboratory of Natural Organic Matter (NOM-Lab) at Southwest University (SWU).
